# F-60: `Delegates`, part 1: the start, the finish by the `result`, held reports

## What was built

Part 1 of F-59 new-9's split, taken from the local branch `framework/f-59-delegates-code`
(commit `f44ac25`), with part 3, the kill path, removed:

- a new app `caf_delegates`: `Delegates` (the start, the brief through `Stream`, the finish by
  the turn's `result`, `held/3`, `deliver/2` refused as `:no_delivery`, a `starting` row
  settled after a restart), `Delegates.Table` and `Delegates.Turn`;
- `Stream.Fold` keeps the `result` event's text in `last_result`; the headless stand-in writes
  `done <uuid>` as that text and holds every turn while a file `hold` is in its directory.

What removing part 3 took: `Delegates` no longer takes `transcripts:` and no longer calls
`Sessions.resume/6`; `exited/3`, `resume/3`, the second-kill text and `Turn.resumed/0` and
`Turn.message/1` are gone, and `caf_transcripts` is no longer a dependency. A process gone before
its turn's `result` leaves the row `running` with no report, with a comment naming the later
unit. `Delegates.Table` keeps the `resumes` and `resume_uuid` columns and counts `resuming`
against the cap, so the kill path's rows need no change of the file.

Tests: the branch's start test and its restart test as they were; the two kill tests are
replaced by one that checks an exit before the `result` leaves the row `running`, holds
nothing, and writes nothing again. `Turn`'s resume test is replaced by one of the brief's own
turn.

## Findings

### new-1. A start answered `:unknown` had no wake at its deadline

What happened: a start's row stays `starting` when `Sessions` answers `{:unknown, _}`, for
`Settled` to decide by `Sessions`' row or, with none, at the row's deadline. The branch set a
wake at each deadline only when `Delegates` starts. A row cut off while it runs waited for some
other cue past its deadline.

What I did: `answered/3` sets the same wake (`wake/2`) for an unknown answer as `init` does for
each `starting` row.

What the design should change: nothing.

### new-2. Part 1 is 548 changed lines apart from tests

What happened: F-59 new-9 estimated part 1 at about 430 lines. It is 538 in the code (with its
docs) and 10 in the README.

What I recommend: split it at its seam, as two units:
1. `Delegates.Table`, `Delegates.Turn`, `apps/caf_delegates/mix.exs` declaring only `exqlite`
   (and `caf_test` for tests), and the `Stream.Fold` change, with `Turn`'s tests: about 230
   lines.
2. `Delegates`, the stand-in's change, the README, and `delegates_test.exs`, with the mix file's
   umbrella dependencies: about 315 lines.

Or allow this unit at 548, since the lines past 500 are docs. The code here is the whole of
part 1 and passes the checks above either way.
