# F-137: interactive delegates

A delegate started `interactive: true` runs Claude Code's terminal interface, as a main
session does, so a person can attach and type. The first try stopped: a brief had no way into
a new interactive session, since the waiter exists only after a turn has ended and the `Inlet`
refuses a long or multi-line text. The brief's "Decided after the first try" settles it with a
new transport, `launch`: the first row to an interactive session is the start's prompt
argument.

## What was built

- **The model first.** `Delivery.tla`: a waiter connects only once a turn of its process has
  ended (`turned`; its `Init` let one connect before any turn ran); `typed` takes only rows not
  in `Long`; and `launch`, a transport whose hand-on is the start itself (`Launch(i, p)`, with
  the process the start makes), marked before it, a refused start undoing the mark, settled by
  the transcript's user line as `waiter` is. `Arrives` now excepts `Held`: a long row to a
  session whose waiter is gone after its turns waits for a person's prompt. New invariant
  `NoLongTyped`; new configurations `Delivery_Launch` and the breaks `Break_Delivery_NoLaunch`
  (a start carries no row: the long brief never arrives, `Arrives` fails) and
  `Break_Delivery_TypeLong`. `Delegate.tla`: `Interactive` delegates whose brief and word to go
  on are marked by launch and carried by the start, a person's turns (`MaxPersonTurns`), and
  the report read from the turn the brief began; new invariant `ReportAnswersTheBrief`,
  `Delegate_Interactive`, and `Break_Delegate_LastReply` (the transcript's last reply, a
  person's later turn's, taken as the report). Every other instancing spec (`Delegate`'s
  parent, `Move`, `Team`, `Stream`) gives `Long = {}`, `Launched = {}` and `turned` true, so
  they check what they checked before (new-1).
- **`ClaudeCLI.prompted/5`**: a wrapper that reads the prompt from a file with the host's `cat`
  into the last argument, after `--`, removes the file, and execs Claude Code; and
  `ClaudeCLI.prompt_max/0`, 512 KiB.
- **`Sessions`**: an interactive start's `prompt:` goes to `<dtach dir>/<name>.prompt` (mode
  0600) and the hold runs the wrapper; with `inlet:`, an interactive start in a worktree first
  answers the trust dialog by label, then confirms as before. A dialog still there at the
  deadline leaves the row `unknown`, reason `trust_dialog`.
- **`Inlet.choose/5`**: a dialog answered by its option's label: the screen must show that
  dialog; the keys from `Screen.option_keys/2` as one command; the option seen selected; Enter
  as its own command.
- **`Mailbox`**: `launch/4` marks the first due row `sending` by `launch` for a start that must
  end by a deadline, and answers its text with the row's mark on a last line; `launched/3`
  takes the start's answer. `Transport.Launch` settles it; a row whose start's answer has not
  come is in memory (`launching`) and no evidence settles it before. After a restart, the
  evidence decides: no process of the start and no line, due again.
- **`Transcripts.Fold.reply_to/2`**: the reply of the turn a marked prompt began, and whether
  a later user entry (a prompt, or a notification that starts a turn, as a waiter's text) has
  closed it.
- **`Delegates`**: `start_delegate(…, interactive: true)` (and `MCP`'s `start_delegate` and
  `run_delegate` with `interactive`): role `parent`, kind `delegate`; the brief stored, marked
  by launch, carried by the start; the report from the transcript once the brief's turn has
  ended (closed, or the session file reads it ended through `Registry`); a resume that carries
  the word to go on (or the brief again) by launch; `launch/2` answers role `parent` for it, so
  `Mover` resumes it as a main session. A headless delegate is unchanged.
- **The stand-in**: `ScreenStandIn` runs the argument after `--` as its first turn and records
  it (`prompts/1`), and with `trust: true` draws 2.1.322's trust dialog first, moved by Down
  and Up and answered by Enter.
- Tests: `Inlet.choose` on a real tmux server; `Mailbox`'s launch over real `Sessions`, dtach
  and tmux (a two-line prompt through the file, settled by its user line; a refused start and
  a restart's cut both due again); an interactive delegate end to end (trust answered, brief as
  the prompt, the report the brief's reply though a person's later turn replied after it); and
  a brief too long for a launch refused. `docs/design.md` ("Delivery", "Interactive
  delegates") and `README.md`'s status and charts.

## What was measured

- The argument size: on this macOS, `getconf ARG_MAX` is 1 048 576 (arguments and environment
  together). One argument of 900 000 bytes, read by `$(cat …)` and passed to `exec`, went
  through whole; one of 1 100 000 bytes failed (`E2BIG`). The launch keeps half: 524 288
  bytes, and `Delegates` refuses a brief over that less 200 bytes for the mark.
- The model: `Delivery_Launch` 1 181 states, 8 s; `Delegate_Interactive` 15 822 states, 14 s;
  `Break_Delivery_NoLaunch` fails at depth 3, `Break_Delivery_TypeLong` at depth 4,
  `Break_Delegate_LastReply` at depth 17.

## What I ran into

- An interactive delegate is already expressible in `Sessions`: role `parent` (how it runs) and
  kind `delegate` (its place in the tree) are separate, and `Mailbox` picks a transport by
  `Agents.headless?/1`, by role. So the waiter, the typed write and the SIGTERM stop come with
  the role, and `Mover` needed only `Delegates.launch/2` to answer the row's role.
- A waiter's text into an ended turn is a user line (`Stop hook blocking error …`) that the
  fold reads as a notification, not a prompt: had the report's reader closed a turn only at a
  prompt, a message taken right after the brief's turn would have given the report. It closes
  the turn at any user entry that is not a tool's result or meta.
- The order of the trust answer and the confirmation is not known for the real release: the
  stand-in writes its session file before the dialog. `Sessions` answers the dialog before it
  confirms, so either order works (new-2).

## Findings

### new-1. A resumed main session's waiter, and a long row to it, are still modeled as before

What happened: `Delivery.tla` now lets a waiter connect only after a turn of its process has
ended, and the `typed` write refuses a long row. `Move.tla`, `Team.tla` and `Delegate.tla`'s
parent instance it with `turned` always true and `Long = {}`, so they still assume a waiter at
once after a resume, and a typed report of any length. Why: changing them is a remodel of each
spec, beyond this unit. In fact a main session resumed by `Mover` with nothing launched has no
waiter until a turn ends, and a report (long, as reports are) to it waits for a person's
prompt; so does a report to a main session after a restart cut its waiter. What I did: kept
their checks as they were, said so in each instance. What the design should change: a
resume by `Mover` carries the first due row by `launch`, as a delegate's resume does, and
those specs instance `turned` and `Long` from their own state.

### new-2. The trust dialog's place in a real start is not measured

What happened: `Sessions` answers the trust dialog before it confirms a start, which works
whether Claude Code 2.1.322 writes its session file before or after the dialog. The fixture
shows the dialog; nothing shows when the session file appears, nor whether a resume on another
account (a move) shows it again. What I did: answered it by label at each check until the
deadline, and took an idle or running screen as a folder trusted before. What the design
should change: the fixture capture script records the session file's appearance relative to
the dialog, and a resume in a folder the account has not trusted.

### new-3. A parent's fixes through `Stream` do not reach an interactive delegate

What happened: `Delegates.fix_state/3` and `fix/5` read the delegate's `Stream` row; an
interactive delegate has none, so they answer `{:unknown, :not_followed}`, and an interrupt
or an answer to its permission request has no way in. Why: those fixes are a headless
session's (stream lines). What I did: nothing; the design says so. What the design should
change: an interactive delegate's interrupt (Escape through the `Inlet`) and its permission
answers (through `Intake`'s held `PermissionRequest`, as a main session's) are a unit of their
own.

### new-4. The report's reader reads the whole transcript at each cue

What happened: `Delegates` reads an interactive delegate's transcript from byte 0 at each cue
while it runs, to find the brief's marked line and its turn. Why: the launch row's offset is
`Mailbox`'s, and a resume's mark is later in the same file. What I did: kept it simple; a
delegate's transcript is read until its report, then not again. What the design should change:
`Delegates` keeps the offset of the marked line once found, and reads from it.
