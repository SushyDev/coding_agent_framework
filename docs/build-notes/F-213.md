# F-213: the instructions say to force a compile after merging main

## What was built

One line in `CLAUDE.md`, under the gates: after merging or rebasing `main`, compile with
`--force` in both environments before the gates.

## What was measured

Nothing new. Since F-208, `--warnings-as-errors` covers the test environment too. After several
merges, `_build` kept a struct's old type, and its "incompatible types" warning failed the gates
until a forced compile cleared it. Every brief since then has carried this line; now it is in the
instructions every agent reads.

## Findings

None.
