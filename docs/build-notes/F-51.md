# F-51: `Stream.tla` models a resume and the end; each process gets its own FIFO

Two parts. The first (`b07819a`) modeled a resume and the end in `Stream.tla` and stopped before
the code: with a restart and a resume, a claim left waiting at the exit went into the resumed
process beside the resend, and the two lines mixed (new-1). The reviewer accepted every
recommendation in new-1 to new-6. The second part models them, writes them into "Headless
delegates", and builds them in `ClaudeCLI`, `Stream`, `Stream.Fold` and `Sessions`.

## What was built

First part, in `Stream.tla`:

- A resume: `FollowAgain` (the row followed again) and `ProcStarts` (Sessions starts the
  resumed process). A message's lines are `<<m, gen>>`; one found undelivered is marked again,
  first, as a new line under its same uuid (`UuidOf`). `heard` is the transcript across
  processes, and `DropMsg` is Claude Code's drop of a user line whose uuid it holds.
- A claim command writes a message line in two parts (`CatBegins`, `CatEnds`), since a message
  is longer than the pipe's atomic size, and opens its FIFO only while a process reads it.
- The end: `EndMarked`, `EndClaimed`, `TakeEnd`, and `ReportDelivered`, the parent's part.
- Checks `EndNeverCuts`, `EndAfterReport`, `ResentOrGone`, `IdleReturns`, `TakenOnce`; Breaks
  `KeepIdle` and `NoReportWait`.

Second part:

- **`Stream.tla`**, the six fixes:
  - new-1: `cfifo` is the FIFO a claim command names, set at `Claim` to the row's resume count;
    `Reads(l)` holds only while the process that reads that FIFO lives (process `n` reads
    `in.<n-1>`). A claim left waiting at an exit times out and never reaches a later process.
  - new-2: a death leaves `cutEnd` (the log ends without a newline), and the next event written
    joins that line (`joined`). `FollowAgain` ends it with a newline, as one `"cut"` line.
  - new-3: `HeldTold` is Sessions telling Stream once it held the process; `telling` is its
    memory of that, set by `ProcStarts` (and at `Init`) and lost at a restart. `Mark` sets
    `seen` too.
  - new-5: `EndMarked` waits for the report (Sessions'), the turn's end, and no message under way
    or found undelivered; `Mark` waits for no end marked. `endLost` is a ghost.
  - new-6: `FollowAgain` sets `idle`, as before.
  - `Handle` delivers only a row `"writing"`: with a FIFO per process no replay comes for a row
    found undelivered, so the first part's clause for one is gone.
  - New invariants `EndLosesNoMessage` and `NoJoinedEvent`. `UndeliveredWasNotTaken` holds
    again in its first, stronger form, without "while gone": "undelivered" is final. `Unseen`
    now needs a restart that cut the tell.
  - New Breaks, each with its configuration: `OneFifo` (`NeverMalformed`), `NoCutEnd`
    (`NoJoinedEvent`), `NoHeldSeen` (`ResentOrGone`), `NoWriteSeen` (`ReplayedOrFound`),
    `MsgAfterEnd` (`EndNeverCuts`). `NewUuid` and its configuration are gone (new-8).
  - Configurations: `Stream_Resume` (a message, a request, a restart, a resume) and
    `Stream_Resumes` (two messages, a restart, a resume), fast; `slow/Stream_ResumeTwice` (two
    messages, a restart, two resumes), the run that failed `UndeliveredWasNotTaken` in the first
    part. The design ones also check the two new invariants.
- **`docs/design.md`**, "Headless delegates": the FIFO per process and the wrapper's new
  argument; how `Stream` knows a process was started; the follow again's newline and its turn;
  a claim names one process's FIFO; "undelivered" is final and the same uuid is defense in
  depth; who checks the report before the end, and what `Stream` refuses; the drop of a known
  uuid among the pinned-release checks. The spec's header and `READING.md` say the same.
- **`ClaudeCLI.wrapper/4`** takes the FIFO's path: `d=$1 f=$2; shift 2`, then `exec 3<>"$f"`.
- **`Stream.Fold`**: `resumes` and `ending` in the fold. `again/1` is `FollowAgain` (the next
  FIFO, `idle` set, `live_seen`, `session_id` and `ending` cleared), `held/1` is `HeldTold`,
  `fifo/1` names `in.<resumes>`. `mark/3` sets `live_seen` (the write's live check) and, for an
  end, `ending`. `check/2`: a message is refused as `:ending` once an end is marked, as
  `{:undelivered, uuid}` while another message is undelivered, and a used uuid only unless its
  message is undelivered (the resend); an end is refused while the turn runs, a message is under
  way (`:message_waiting`) or one is undelivered, or an end is already marked.
- **`Stream`**: `follow/5` answers `{:ok, %{dir: dir, fifo: fifo}}`. It reads a row not yet
  seen gone first, live seen or not, and refuses a live socket; then it makes the FIFO and ends a
  cut last line with a newline (`tail -c 1`), so it never touches a log a live process writes.
  `held/3` is the tell. A claim names `Fold.fifo/1` of the row.
- **`Sessions`**: the wrapper gets the follow's FIFO; a hold that answers `{:ok, _}` calls
  `Stream.held/3` (one that answers unknown does not: a process may start later, and a read sees
  it then). A delegate's stop needs `:report_delivered`, the caller's word on the report, and
  refuses anything else as `{:report, word}`.
- **Tests**, on the stand-ins:
  - `Stream`: a message written to a process that never reads (`sleep`) is found undelivered
    when it is killed, with the turn left running; a command left writing to `in.0` waits there
    through the resume; the resumed process reads `in.1`; another message and an end are refused
    `{:undelivered, uuid}`; the resend under the same uuid is taken once, and the old command's
    line is still on `in.0` (new-1, new-4, new-5, new-6). A message ending with a cut line, then
    an end: the resumed stand-in's `system`/`init` is read, with one problem (new-2). A process
    that exits before any read is seen gone once `held/3` tells (new-3). A message after an end
    is refused `:ending` (new-5).
  - `Sessions`: a delegate's stop is refused without `:report_delivered`; the resume test checks
    `in.0` and `in.1`; a delegate whose program exits at once is seen gone by `Stream`, since
    its hold was told.
  - `Stream.Fold`: the follow again, the end's and the resend's checks, the tell.
  - Each new test was run against the code with its fix taken out (one FIFO for every process;
    no newline; no tell) and failed.

## What I ran into

- A follow again used to read the row only when it had been seen live. With the newline it
  must never write a log a live process writes, so it now reads every row not yet seen gone,
  and a live socket is refused whether or not it was seen live before.
- `NoCutEnd` needs two messages: after one, the resumed process has nothing to write, so no
  event joins the cut line.
- Commit signing (1Password's `op-ssh-sign`) failed with "failed to fill whole buffer" for
  every commit in this session; see "Gates".

## Findings

### new-1. A claim waiting at the exit goes into the resumed process beside the resend

What happened (first part): TLC's trace, one message, a restart and a resume: a claim command
started while no process read the FIFO waited, and the resumed process's open released it, so
it went in beside the resend. The two lines mixed (`NeverMalformed`), or the waiting line went in
and the row stayed undelivered though Claude Code ran it (`UndeliveredWasNotTaken`).

Why: a resume reused the FIFO's path, so a command from before the exit could write into the
next process.

What I did: each process has its own FIFO `in.<n>`, named by the row's resume count, which the
follow makes and the wrapper is given; every claim names the current one. `Break_Stream_OneFifo`
fails on the first part's trace; both failing runs pass. Old FIFOs stay: removing one would let a
command that has not reached its `open` create a regular file by that name.

What the design should change: done, in "Held, apart from the framework" and "Writes are
lines".

### new-2. A cut line at the log's end joins the resumed process's first line

What happened (first part): a resume appends to the same log, so the resumed process's first
event, `system`/`init` in the stand-in, joined the line a death cut, and a resume would wait for
that init for good.

What I did: the follow again ends a cut last line with a newline before the new process starts,
after it has checked the old socket is not live. Modeled as `cutEnd`; `Break_Stream_NoCutEnd`.

What the design should change: done, in "Events are read from an offset".

### new-3. A process that exits before a read sees it live is never seen gone

What happened (first part): `ExitSeen` needs `live_seen`, which only a read set, so a resumed
process that exited before such a read left its row neither live nor gone.

What I did: `Sessions` tells `Stream` once a hold answers held (`Stream.held/3`), and a write's
mark sets it. Modeled as `HeldTold` and in `Mark`, with `Break_Stream_NoHeldSeen` and
`Break_Stream_NoWriteSeen`. A window stays: a restart between the hold and the tell, then an
exit before any read. `Unseen` names it. It is benign now: no message is written there without
a write's check, and a follow again no longer needs the exit seen (it reads the socket itself).

What the design should change: nothing more.

### new-4. 2.1.322 may run a known uuid again when its turn went unanswered

What happened (first part): `skipDuplicate` may run again a line whose turn the transcript
shows cut off.

What I did: with a FIFO per process the framework does not rely on the drop: "undelivered" is
final and the resend is never a duplicate. The drop is on the list of checks against each pinned
release.

What the design should change: done.

### new-5. The end's wait for the turn is defense in depth behind the report

What happened (first part): no Break could fail on the end's wait for the turn, since the report
is delivered only while idle.

What I did: `Sessions` checks the report before a delegate's end, by the caller's word
(`:report_delivered`) until `Delegates` holds the parent's row. `Stream` refuses the end while
the turn runs, a message is under way or found undelivered, or an end is already marked, and
refuses a message once an end is marked. `Break_Stream_MsgAfterEnd` fails on the last.

What the design should change: done, in "Ending".

### new-6. A follow again keeps `idle`, so a delegate that exited during a turn is never written

What I did: `Fold.again/1` sets `idle`; the `Stream` test's process exits during the turn its
message started, and the resend after the resume is accepted.

What the design should change: done, in "Events are read from an offset".

### new-7. The end's wait for no undelivered message is defense in depth in the spec, not in the code

What happened: an end marked while a message is found undelivered never fails: an undelivered
message was marked after the last `result`, so the report is not delivered and the end waits
anyway (3,346 states, ok).

Why: the spec takes the report as delivered only when it is. In the code it is the caller's
word, so `Stream`'s refusal is what stops an end that would drop the resend.

What I did: no Break for it; the spec's header names `Stream`'s end guards as defense in depth
behind `Sessions`' wait for the report, kept for a caller whose word is wrong.

What the design should change: once `Delegates` holds the parent's row, `Sessions` should check
the report there and the caller's word goes away.

### new-8. `Break_Stream_NewUuid` no longer fails

What happened: with a FIFO per process, a resend under a new uuid never runs twice, since the
first line never went in (the first part measured this on a copy).

What I did: removed the `NewUuid` Break and its configuration. `TakenOnce` stays in every design
configuration, and the header names the same uuid and the drop as defense in depth.

What the design should change: nothing.

### new-9. A fold of an earlier build loads as a new fold

What happened: `Stream.Table` loads a stored fold only when its keys are exactly those of
`Fold.new/0`, and loads any other as a new fold. This unit adds `resumes` and `ending`, so a row
an earlier build wrote would load with offset 0 and its log would be handled again from the
start.

Why: the load is total by design, and nothing so far changed the fold's shape.

What I did: nothing; nothing is deployed yet.

What the design should change: say how a stored row's shape changes across builds: a version
in the row, or a load that fills a missing field with its default and refuses an unknown one.

### new-10. The two-resume configuration is slow

What happened: two messages, a restart and two resumes take about 30 s, and two messages, a
request, a restart and a resume 34 s, past the fast limit.

What I did: `slow/Stream_ResumeTwice` keeps the first; the fast ones split it into a message
with a request, and two messages, each with one restart and one resume.

What the design should change: nothing.

### new-11. `CaptureScreenTest` failed once, on a blank screen

What happened: in one of six `gate-test` runs, F-48's "writes each state with and without
escapes, scrubbed, with a manifest" failed: `assert escaped =~ "\e["` read a capture of empty
lines. The other five runs passed. This unit does not touch the script or its test.

Why: not looked into; the capture may be taken before the pane has drawn, which a busy machine
makes likelier.

What I did: nothing beyond running the gate three more times.

What the design should change: nothing; the test's wait should be checked by whoever next
touches `bin/capture-screen`.
