# F-45: the umbrella's rules, from F-37's, F-42's and F-43's findings (design)

## What was built

Design only, no code. "The parts" takes the findings of the umbrella move and its follow-ups:

- An app declares, directly, every app it uses and only those, and `gate-deps` checks it:
  Elixir alone accepts a module from any app in the dependency tree (F-37 new-2, F-43 new-3).
- A test of a Tracker source, or of two parts together, belongs to the higher app (F-37 new-1).
- Test aids that use no part above `caf_shell` live in `caf_test`; setup a part's tests share
  with a higher app's tests lives in that part's `test/support/`, compiled only for tests
  (F-42 new-1).

F-37's new-3 (one way of opening the SQLite tables, a `caf_sqlite`) and F-42's new-2 (`hold!`
written twice) are left for units of their own.
