# F-248: a stop's exit press is bounded by the press chain

## What was built

F-245's new-2. A stop of a row on a dialog presses "No, exit" (F-231). `Sessions.stop/3` waited
its own 30 s (and `Mains.end_main/3` a fixed 90 s), which is less than the press's 65 040 ms
at the default `see`; the press's own answer then came after the stop's row deadline.

- `Sessions.stop_bound/2` (pure): `steps + press_wait`. `stop/3` and `end_main/3` take
  `timeout:` as the stop's own steps (default 30 s) and add the press's wait: the caller asks
  `press_call/2` first and waits `stop_bound(steps, press) + 30 000` of slack; the server gives
  a row on a dialog the deadline `steps + press allowance`, so a press answered late, within
  its bound, ends `ok`.
- `within:` is a caller's own deadline (ms from the call's arrival on `Sessions`' clock). It
  wins: a press still under way at it answers `{:unknown, {:cut, :end_press}}` through a timer
  message, so the caller's call is answered before its wait and never exits. The row stays on
  its dialog; the press may have gone, and a later look finds the truth.
- `Mains.end_main/3` with no `timeout:` asks `Sessions` the press (`:end_wait`) and waits
  `stop_bound(30 000, press) + 60 000`; with `timeout:` (the operator's deadline) it passes
  `within: timeout - 1 000` so the stop's answer comes back inside it.
- `Mover` passes `within:` equal to its `stop:` (the move's deadline). A move's stop never
  presses a dialog, so nothing changes for it. `Delegates` passes its `@call` as steps; a
  delegate is headless and has no dialog, so no press is added.

## The chain

| Bound | Derived as | At `see` 10 000 | At `see` 2 000 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| the press's wait, `Sessions.press_wait/1` | the `Inlet`'s press bound + 5 000 | 65 040 | 49 040 |
| a stop's bound, `Sessions.stop_bound/2` | steps (30 000) + the press's wait | 95 040 | 79 040 |
| the stop's call | the stop's bound + 30 000 slack | 125 040 | 109 040 |
| `Mains.end_main/3`'s wait | the stop's bound + 60 000 slack | 155 040 | 139 040 |

Callers with a deadline of their own: the operator's `end_main` (`timeout:`) and `Mover`'s stop
(`stop:`, the move's). Others (`Delegates`, the app's `Run`) name only steps.

## What was measured

Tests on the stand-in: a stop whose program is stopped (SIGSTOP) for 1.5 s, with steps of
300 ms, ends `ok`; with `within: 300` it answers `{:unknown, {:cut, :end_press}}` in well under
5 s; `stop_bound/2` derives as the table says. `sessions_dialog_test.exs` ran alone: 19 pass.

## What I ran into

The model's `Dialog.tla` has no clock, so the cut is not in it: a cut is the stop's unknown,
which the spec already allows after an exit press.

## Findings

### new-1. A cut press leaves the row on its dialog

What happened: a press cut by `within:` may still have gone, and the row, still `dialog`, is
read again by the look. Why: the cut answers the caller, not the process. What I did: left it.
What the design should change: nothing, unless a cut should mark the row.
