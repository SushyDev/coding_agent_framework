# F-186: the limit's healer (`Limits`)

The brief's item 1 (F-185's "What is left" of its item 1) and item 3, against `Limit.tla`, on
the shape F-185's new-1 and new-2 decided. Item 1 alone ran past 1,000 changed lines, so as the
brief says this unit lands it and stops before item 2, the moves. No test started the real
Claude Code, and nothing was measured by hand.

## What was built

- **`Limits`** (`caf_recoveries`), one healer per host, after `Sessions`, `Mailbox`, the
  `Watch` and `Mains` in the tree. It reads the `sessions`, `transcripts`, `stream`, `intake`
  and `watch` cues, and keeps one timer to the earliest row's reset plus a margin (2 min) or
  `usage_credits` deadline (10 min). It is given its readers and acts as functions
  (`transcripts:`, `folds:`, `look:`, `word:`, `resume:`, `press:`), which the tree builds from
  `Transcripts.rows/3`, `Stream.rows/2`, `Inlet.look/4`, `Mailbox.notify/4` (kind `notice`,
  under its key) and `Mains.resume_main/3`. Per agent, by `Limit.tla`'s actions:
  - `Read`: the newest record (an interactive agent's transcript fold `limit`, parsed by
    `Usage.Limit.parse/1`; a headless one's last result with `api_error:
    "usage_limit_reached"` and the fold's `rate_limit_event`) onto the row, step `look`; a step
    `unknown` or `prompt` stays. Unread evidence decides nothing (`Current`).
  - `Continued` (the fold's `followed`, or a headless turn after the result): the limit cleared,
    its count of go-ons kept; `Ended` (a reply ended the turn): the count goes.
  - `look`, `own`, `credits`, `press`, `unknown`, `idle`, `send`, `word`, `prompt`, as the spec
    names them; the margin stands for `Overdue`'s and `GoOn`'s "the reset passed".
  - The one shape: `limited` recorded per agent and limit, shown only; `limit_menu`
    (`press_stop`) and `limit_reset` (`go_on`) recorded, checked against the list and its cap
    (a fold of this healer's rows in `Acts`), claimed, acted once, settled. A press after a
    person's "go on" is a new claim (the row's `menu` count is in the claim's state). A person's
    `usage_credits` answer is pressed as theirs, claimed with `detail: %{by: who}`, whatever the
    list says. A claim a restart cut makes a press `unknown` and a word a `limit` prompt. A
    press refused because the screen moved reads it again (`Press`'s else); refused for any
    other reason it pressed nothing and its state is claimed, so it is a `limit` prompt. The
    claim's state also takes the record's line, so a new hit's menu is a new claim.
- **`Sessions.Limit`**, the row's field as one pure module: its steps, the prompt it shows, its
  options, a person's answers, and what the row keeps once the limit clears.
- **`Sessions`**: `put_limit/4` takes `from:` (the step the caller read; another step now is
  refused `{:moved, step}`) and a map without `until` (the counts alone);
  `answer_limit/6`, a person's answer checked and written in one step; `press_limit/4`, the
  `Inlet`'s checked press of kind `usage_limit_menu` in a task, only from the step `press` and
  for an option the row read.
- **`Prompts`**: the `limit` prompt (answers `go_on`, `leave`) and the `usage_credits` prompt
  (the menu's stop or its credits, by label; the healer's deadline), from the row's step,
  answered through `Sessions.answer_limit/6`.
- **`Watch.default_remedies/0`**: `limited` (none), `limit_menu` (`press_stop`, 20),
  `limit_reset` (`go_on`, 20).
- **`Limit.tla`**: the header names the record as the transcript's line and the stream's event,
  never `StopFailure` (F-185's new-4); the code it models is named; `Leave(a)` and the step
  `left` model the `limit` prompt's "leave it", which the design lists and the spec lacked, and
  `NoAgentLeftLimited` allows it.
- **Docs**: "Usage limits" (what F-186 built), "Prompts" (the two kinds), the parts table and
  the cue table (`Limits` on its five topics; the `intake:<host>:<account>` row now names its
  readers).

## Findings

### new-1. A headless delegate's limited turn is taken as its report

What happened: Claude Code headless ends a turn at a limit with a `result` (`is_error: true`,
`api_error: "usage_limit_reached"`) and exits 1 at once (F-181). `Delegates.Turn.next/2`
takes any result naming the brief as `{:finished, result}`, so the delegate's report to its
parent is the limit's text, and its work ends there. `Limit.tla`'s `Deliver` hands the word to a
gone process by `launch` under a lease, but a delegate's resumes are `Delegates`', so `Limits`
resumes none (the tree's `resume:` is `:none` for a delegate). Why: F-181 measured the result
after `Delegates` was built; F-185 made the row `limited` for every reader but `Delegates`.
What I did: nothing in `Delegates`; `Limits` reads a headless agent's limit onto its row, so
`limited` shows, and a delegate the report ended is not acted on. What the design should
change: `Delegates.Turn.next/2` reads a result with `api_error: "usage_limit_reached"` as
`:limited`, not finished: no report, the brief's turn still open. At the reset, `Limits` stores
the word and hands the resume to `Delegates` (a `resume:` it serves, under its lease, the
word its first row, not counted as the resume after a kill), as the design's table says.

### new-2. A limit prompt's "leave" was in the design and not in the spec

What happened: "Usage limits" gives the `limit` prompt three answers (go on, move, leave it);
`Limit.tla` had `AnswerLimit` and `AnswerMove` only, and `NoAgentLeftLimited` would fail with a
"leave". What I did: added `Leave(a)` and the step `left`, and `NoAgentLeftLimited` now holds
up to a person's leave; checked as above. What the design should change: nothing more, unless
"leave" is not meant to stop every remedy on that limit; then it says what still acts.

### new-3. A person's prompt is the row's step, so a record read late keeps it

What happened: `Limit.tla`'s `Read` keeps `pp` open but moves the step to `look`, so the model's
framework may read and press while the prompt is open. The row has one step, and the prompt is
that step, so the code keeps `prompt` (and `unknown`) through a record read late and acts on
nothing until the person answers or a turn runs. Why: one field, one phase. What I did: the
stricter reading; it acts in fewer states, so the safety properties hold for it, and a prompt
is an allowed end of `NoAgentLeftLimited`. What the design should change: `Read` keeps
`prompt` as it keeps `unknown`, so spec and code agree.

### new-4. The healer's asks for these kinds are the `limit` prompt where the spec names one

What happened: on F-182's shape a refused, unknown or cut act is asked through `Watch.ask/4`
(a `remedy` prompt). `Limit.tla` names the person's prompt for those outcomes (an unknown press,
a word that did not go, a claim a restart cut): the `limit` prompt. Asking both would show one
wait twice. What I did: those outcomes are the `limit` prompt on the row; only a kind off the
list or past its cap is asked in the `Watch`, as for every healer. A word's claim cut by a
restart is the `limit` prompt, where the spec stores it again under its key: stricter, and the
key would make either safe. What the design should change: "Metrics and healing" says a
limit kind's ask is its `limit` prompt where `Limit.tla` names one.
