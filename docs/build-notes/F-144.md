# F-144: F-140's follow-ups, and the last of F-143's

The brief took F-140's new-2 to new-6 and F-143's new-1, new-3 to new-5 as decided. Each is
built, the protocols first in `Dialog.tla` and `Team.tla`.

## What was built

- **The models.**
  - `Dialog.tla`: the press runs in a task (`Press`), and its answer comes back as a message
    (`Pressed`), lost with the task at a restart. A press a restart cut is `cut`; a person's
    `Clear` (the pane still shows that dialog) makes it `cleared`, so it may be answered once
    more, and a press of it cut again is `unknown`. A person's `Stop` of a row on a dialog
    sends SIGTERM; the process exits or outlives it. `AtMostOnce` is now "at most once, and
    once more only after a clear". New break `ClearTwice` (a press cut after its clear may be
    cleared again) fails `AtMostOnce`. The answer's `Acts` row is not modeled apart: its claim's
    states are the marks.
  - `Team.tla`: the row keeps the end asked (`ending`, durable). `MainEnds` either ends the row
    in its step or, under the new constant `StopsUnknown`, leaves the mark when the stop
    answers unknown; `EndTerm` (the late SIGTERM takes, or `Recoveries` ends it) and
    `EndSettled` (the settle ends a marked row once its process is gone) finish it. `Mailbox`
    marks no row to a marked session (Delivery's `stopping`), and no resume acts on one
    (`CanAct`). New properties `NoResumeOfEnding` and `EndAskedEnds`; new breaks
    `ResumeEnding` and `EndInMemory` (the mark held by the call, so a restart forgets it). New
    configuration `Team_MainEndsAsked`; `Team_Operator` moved to `slow/` (F-143's new-3).
- **`Screen`** (F-140's new-5): the capture `imports-long-path`, paths scrubbed to `/work`, is a
  fixture. 2.1.322 shortens a path wider than the pane in its middle with `…` on one line; the
  reading names such paths in `shortened:` beside `paths:`. The row's `dialog` keeps it.
- **`Sessions`**:
  - the press in a task (new-2): `answer_dialog/5` and the app's callback mark the answer
    `pressing`, then `Inlet.choose/5` runs in a linked task; its answer comes back as a
    message, which writes the mark, settles the act and replies to the caller. A row whose
    press is under way is not read until it answers (see new-2 below);
  - the clear (new-3): a restart turns `pressing` into `cut` and settles its act unknown.
    `clear_dialog/4` reads the pane, refuses unless it shows that dialog with its first option
    selected, marks it `cleared` and clears the cut act in `Acts`. A cleared answer may be given
    once more; a press of it cut again is `unknown`, never cleared again;
  - the stop on a dialog (new-4): `stop/3` of a `dialog` row sends SIGTERM to its held
    program's record and waits, as a stop does, for the process to be gone; the row is then
    `stopped`;
  - the answer as an act (new-6): each answer claims in `Acts`, act `answer_startup`, actor the
    caller's `by:` (default `"person"`, the operator's tool passes the operator) or `"app"` for
    the callback, state `%{start: started_at, dialog: kind, mark: from}`, and is settled by the
    press's answer (`Acts.answered/4`);
  - the end kept on the row (F-143's new-4): `end_main` writes `end_asked` with `stopping`; a
    stop that answered unknown keeps it, and the settle of a `stopping` row writes `ended`, not
    `stopped`, once its process is gone. A refusal or a restart's return to `running` takes it
    away. The column is added to older files.
- **`Mains`**: `main_starts` has `resume` (the session id resumed, added if missing; F-143's
  new-5). `fix_state/3` reads a main session's state (status, session id, pid, start time), and
  `resume_main/3` takes `state:`, refused `{:moved, now}` when the row's state is another.
- **`MCP`**: the operator's `resume` of a main session (F-143's new-1) claims `Mains.fix_state/3`
  in `Acts` and calls `Mains.resume_main/3` with that state; its other fixes of a main session
  are still `{:not_built, :main_fix}`. `answer_startup` passes the operator as `by:`.
- **Docs**: `docs/design.md` ("Main sessions", "Startup dialogs", the models, the order), and
  the moduledocs of `Sessions`, `Sessions.Dialog`, `Sessions.Table`, `Screen`, `Mains`,
  `Mains.Starts` and `MCP`.

## What I ran into

- The first test of the press in a task failed: `Sessions` was still held up 5 s. The press was
  in its task, but the periodic look of the same row called `Inlet.look/4`, and the `Inlet`, one
  process, was busy with the press. A row marked `pressing` is now not read until its press
  answers.
- An inverted guard (`pressing? || :pressed`) made every look answer `:pressed` and every start
  wait; the dialog tests caught it at once.
- Under the gate's load the dialog tests failed twice more. A look checked the `pressing` mark
  on the caller's copy of the row (a start's, written before the mark), so a start read the next
  dialog before the trust press's answer came back; `look_at` now reads the row first. And the
  press test held the `Inlet` while a look a cue started could wait on it (new-2); it now waits
  longer for the mark and runs with no periodic look.

## Findings

### new-1. The unit is over 500 changed lines

What happened: 1 137 lines added and 203 removed across 64 files with this note, of which about
40 are the one line each `Team` configuration takes for `StopsUnknown`, and about 60 the
fixture and its manifest. Why: the
brief took nine items, two of them models. What I did: kept each item to its own functions.
What the design should change: nothing.

### new-2. A look of another row waits behind a press in the `Inlet`

What happened: the press runs in a task, and a row being pressed is not read, but the `Inlet`
is one process per host: a look of another waiting row during a press (up to 20 s) waits in
the `Inlet`'s queue, and `Sessions` with it, up to 5 s per look. Why: the `Inlet` serializes
its reads and its keys. What I did: nothing more; it needs two rows on dialogs at once. What
the design should change: say whether `Sessions`' looks run in a task too, or the `Inlet`
reads a pane outside its own process.

### new-3. The clear and the stop on a dialog have no operator tool

What happened: `Sessions.clear_dialog/4` and `Sessions.stop/3` of a row on a dialog are built,
but only the app calls them; the operator answers a startup dialog through `answer_startup` and
can neither clear a cut press nor give up the start. Why: the brief named the mechanisms, not
the tools. What I did: nothing more. What the design should change: an operator tool for each
(`clear_startup(agent, kind)`, and a stop of a start on a dialog), or `end_main` of a main
session on a dialog by the same SIGTERM.

### new-4. A stop a restart cut forgets the end asked

What happened: the mark is written with `stopping`; when a restart finds the process alive and
the stop not late, the row goes back to `running` and the mark goes with it, so the end needs a
second `end_main`. Why: whether the SIGTERM went is not known, and a row `stopping` with a live
process and no stop under way would hold `Mailbox` off it for good. What I did: modeled the end
asked as one step with its SIGTERM, as the stop is, and documented it. What the design should
change: nothing, unless an end cut by a restart should be asked again by the settle once the
session reads idle.

### new-5. `Team.tla` has a constant for the unknown stop

What happened: with the unknown stop in every configuration, `Team_MainEnds` grew from 4 807 to
48 986 distinct states and 69 s, past the fast bound. Why: the mark doubles the main sessions'
states while messages are in flight. What I did: a constant `StopsUnknown`, `TRUE` only in
`Team_MainEndsAsked` and the two new breaks, which check the mark over a restart. What the
design should change: nothing.

### new-6. The answer's claimed state names the row's start

What happened: "state the dialog's mark" alone would refuse the answer to the same dialog in a
later start of the same name (`{:already_claimed, …}`), since marks are reset per start. Why:
`Acts` holds each state once for good. What I did: the state is the row's `started_at`, the
dialog and the mark it is taken from (`none` or `cleared`). The app's callback acts as
`"app"`, a fixed actor name. What the design should change: say whether the app names its
callback's actor.
