# F-179: one inbox of prompts (B6's first part)

An app is about to run every main session and builder on the framework, in the default
permission mode, so its agents ask permission, meet startup dialogs, ask questions and hit the
`Stop` cap. Before this unit only asks and `stop_cap` reached the app's panel, through bridges
the app built by hand, and a held permission request or a startup dialog reached no person
until its hold's deadline. This unit builds B6's front: one reader of every open request for a
person, one answer, and the parent told once.

## What was built

- **The model first.** `Prompt.tla` gains the parent's notice: `Notice(p)` stores one notice
  for the asker's parent, when the asker is a delegate, under the prompt's key, so a read after
  a restart stores none. `ParentToldOnce` (invariant) and `ParentTold` (liveness) on
  `Prompt_Holds`, `Prompt_Owners` and `Prompt_Asks`; `Break_Prompt_NoticeUnkeyed` (the notice
  under a key of its own) fails `ParentToldOnce`. Its header now says what the code is: a
  prompt is its owner's row read by one function, the spec's `st` that function's value, and
  the owner's own guard the claim `Answer` and `AnswerNow` take. `Dialog.tla` and `Team.tla`
  set the new variable in their instances. `Boxes.tla` composes it: a `Notice` of the
  addressee's dialog prompt, across the agent's restart and the whole one, under a new
  constant `Delegated` (the addressee is a delegate whose parent Prompts tells), checked in the
  new `Boxes_Notice` (`ParentToldOnce`, `ParentTold`, `KeptAcrossRestart`, `OneAnswer`,
  `DialogPasses`); `Break_Boxes_NoticeUnkeyed` fails `ParentToldOnce`. `Boxes_Safety` sets
  `Delegated = TRUE` and checks `ParentToldOnce`; `Boxes_Liveness` keeps a main session.
- **`caf_prompts`, the `Prompts` box's app.** `CodingAgentFramework.Prompts`, one process per
  instance, keeps no table: `list/1` is one function of its owners' rows. A held
  `PermissionRequest` (`Intake.Table.waiting/2`, kind `permission`, a subagent's `agent_id`
  as `subagent`), a `Stop` held at the cap (`stop_cap`), an agent row on a startup dialog with
  no press marked (`dialog`, from `Sessions.Agents.dialog/1`), and `Mailbox`'s open asks
  (`ask`). Each prompt names its id (its owner's key: `permission:<id>`, `stop_cap:<id>`,
  `dialog:<agent>:<start>:<dialog>`, `ask:<id>`), status, agent, parent, project, subagent,
  text, options, when it opened and its deadline. `read/3` filters by `agent`, `subtree` and
  `kind`. On each cue of `intake`, `sessions` and `mailbox` it reads again and cues `prompts`
  with the ids that opened, moved or closed (`writes/0`).
- **One answer.** `Prompts.answer/5` (id, choice, who) checks the prompt read now (open, and the
  choice one it offers), then hands it to its owner in the caller's process: `Intake.answer/4`
  with `by:`, `Sessions.answer_dialog/5` with `by:`, `Mailbox.answer/4`, or `Mailbox.withdraw/4`
  for `{:withdraw, reason}`. A prompt answered, withdrawn or gone since, and an owner's refusal
  of a request no longer waiting, are both `{:refused, :settled}`. `answer_startup/6` finds a
  dialog's prompt by agent and dialog for the operator's tool.
- **The parent told once.** For each open prompt whose agent row is a delegate, one
  `Mailbox.notify/4` to its parent, kind `prompt` (new), from the delegate, key `prompt:<id>`:
  the parent reads ``From <delegate> (prompt): waiting on permission: Bash git push (prompt
  permission:41)``. No turn answers it.
- **`Intake`**: each hold's row keeps its `deadline` (a new column, wall-clock ms), and
  `answer/4` takes `by:`, the row's actor (none for a permission and `person` for a cap without
  it, as before).
- **The app's surface**: `CodingAgentFramework.prompts/2` and `answer/4`, the `:prompts` child
  after `Mailbox`, registered in the instance's own `Registry`, its reads and writes in the cue
  table, and the `prompts` row of `subscribe/2`'s table. `MCP`'s `:sessions` option became
  `:prompts`: the operator's `answer_startup` and `withdraw` go through the inbox.
- **Tests.** `apps/caf_prompts/test/prompts_test.exs`, on a real `Intake`, `Mailbox`, `Sessions`,
  `Inlet`, tmux, dtach and the stand-ins: a delegate's permission request listed, filtered,
  cued, told to its parent once across a cue and a restart of the inbox, answered once by
  `allow` with its actor; a `Stop` at the cap as `stop_cap`, `go_on` once; two asks, one
  answered and one withdrawn, each once; a trust dialog drawn by the screen stand-in, pressed
  once by label. The surface's test: the same request on the whole tree, its `prompts` cue to
  the app's subscriber, the notice to `app-main`, `answer/4` once, and the operator's
  `answer_startup` of a dialog never shown refused `settled`.
- Docs: the design's "Prompts" (built so far), the app's surface, the model and B6; the
  README's built list and the apps' chart.

## Findings

### new-1. The design says a `prompts` table; the brief says no copy of the owners' state

What happened: the design's "Prompts" and B6 name a `prompts` table, `open/2`, `answer/3` and
`read/2`, a row with its own status. The brief says the inbox is one function of the parts' own
rows, with no copy of their state.
Why: a table would be a second record of each held request, dialog and ask, which can lag its
owner; the owners already keep every status the design lists but `expired` shown and `cut`.
What I did: followed the brief. `Prompts` has no `open/2` (each owner's own write opens its
prompt) and no table; `Prompt.tla`'s header says its row is the function's value.
What the design should change: "Prompts" and B6 say the box keeps no table and its interface is
`read` and `answer`; the boxes' table's `Owns` for `Prompts` is none.

### new-2. The kinds' names: `ask` and `dialog`, where the design says `question` and `startup`

What happened: the design's table of kinds, and `Prompt.tla`, call an ask `question` and a
startup dialog `startup`; the brief names them `ask` and `dialog`.
What I did: the code uses the brief's names; the specs keep theirs.
What the design should change: one set of names. I recommend the code's (`ask`, `dialog`): they
are the words the app, the tools and the rows already use.

### new-3. `answer/4`, not `answer/3`: who answers is an argument

What happened: the design's surface says `answer(caf, prompt, choice)`; the brief asks for
`answer/4` with who answers.
What I did: `answer/4`. The person's name is the row's actor in `Intake` and the act's actor in
`Acts` for a dialog.
What the design should change: the surface's `answer/4`.

### new-4. An ask's answer does not record who gave it

What happened: `Mailbox`'s answer row is from the operator; `answer/4`'s `who` has nowhere to go
for an ask.
What I did: nothing; a permission's and a dialog's answers record it.
What the design should change: an answer row's actor in `Mailbox`, or one `Acts` row per answer
of every kind, if the app needs to see who answered an ask.

### new-5. An expired prompt is not shown

What happened: the design says a prompt past its deadline with no default is `expired` and
stays shown, since Claude Code no longer reads the hold. The inbox lists `held` and `deciding`
holds only, so a permission request past its deadline leaves the inbox, while Claude Code's own
dialog may still be on the pane.
What I did: left it out; the owner's row says `expired`.
What the design should change: whether an expired permission prompt is shown, until when (the
session's next event?), and what a person's answer to it does (it can only go to the pane).

### new-6. The parent's answer through `steer` is not the inbox's yet

What happened: the design says the asker's parent answers `permission` through `steer`. Today
that is the `answer` fix under `Grants`' claim, which calls `Intake.answer/4` itself.
What I did: left it as it is; a parent's answer and a person's race in `Intake`'s hold, which
lets one in.
What the design should change: nothing now; B5's `steer` hands it to `Prompts.answer/5`, its
claim kept as the edge check (F-167's new-5).

### new-7. A dialog's prompt has no time of its own

What happened: an agent row keeps no time for the dialog it shows; `opened_at` is the row's
`updated_at`, which a press's mark moves.
What I did: documented it.
What the design should change: a `dialog_at` on the agent row, written with the dialog, if the
app sorts the inbox by age.

### new-8. The inbox reads at every waiter's cue

What happened: `Prompts` reads `intake`, and a topic pattern matches every topic that begins
with its words, so each waiter's coming and going (every `Stop`) is a read of the inbox too.
What I did: nothing; a read is three queries.
What the design should change: if it ever costs, `Cues` takes a pattern that ends (`intake:*:*$`).

### new-9. This unit is over 500 changed lines

What happened: about 550 changed lines in the existing apps, specs and docs, and about 800 in
the new app (500 of its module, half of them its docs, and 270 of its tests).
Why: the model, a new app, three owners' small changes, the surface, `MCP` and the tests.
What I did: kept each part apart in the diff.
What the design should change: nothing; B6's estimate was +600.
