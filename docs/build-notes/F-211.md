# F-211: a move's resume has a deadline; the console's lease line says where it was asked

The brief's three items: F-210's new-2 and new-1 as the app decided them. No test started the
real Claude Code.

## What was built

- **The model first** (`Move.tla`). A constant `Unsure` and two variables: `untold`, the
  accounts the check at a move's resume (`usable:`) answers unknown for now, and `waited`, the
  resume's deadline has passed.
  - `CheckUnsure` turns an account's answer unknown, or known again, at any time, and may never
    turn it back: no fairness. `Answer(a)` is `unknown` for an account in `untold`, else the
    flag's `refused` or `ok`. `TargetTaken` is `Answer(target) = "ok"`, so an unknown holds
    `LaunchMarked`, `ResumedWith` and `ResumedBare`.
  - `ResumeWaitEnds` passes the deadline once per move, from the copy on. It is a field of the
    row, so a restart keeps it, and one deadline covers the resume and its way back. Only an
    unknown reads it, so it runs only with `Unsure`: the other configurations keep their state
    spaces.
  - `MoveBack` and `Stranded` take `TakenAsRefused(a)`: the flag's refusal, or an unknown once
    `waited`.
  - **`ResumeEnds`**, liveness: `(phase \in {"stopping", "ending"} /\ copied /\ procs = {}) ~>
    phase \in {"moved", "failed"}`, even when the check never answers again.
  - **Fairness**: a weak fairness on `ResumeWaitEnds`, and one on `Resumed \/ MoveBack \/
    Stranded` as a whole. The `Mover` asks at its interval and acts on each answer, so an answer
    that changes between asks (the app's flag and the check both toggle) does not hold the
    resume. Each step's own fairness is kept.
  - **`Move_Unsure.cfg`** (new): `Flags` and `Unsure`, a prompt, a crash and a restart, with the
    safety properties, `NoResumeOnRefused` and `ResumeEnds`. **`Break_Move_NoResumeWait`** (new)
    has no deadline. The trace: `untold = {a2}` on the target after the copy, never told again,
    and the resume waits for ever. Every other `Move_*` and `Break_Move_*` configuration and
    `slow/Move_Large` set `Unsure = FALSE`.
  - The header's `Mover` and app entries say it.
- **The code** (`Mover`):
  - `resume/2` reads `usable:` first. `{:ok, account}` goes to the launch (`resume_on/3`, the old
    body). A refusal is `answered/4` as before. An unknown goes to `unanswered/3`.
  - Before the deadline, `unanswered/3` asks again at the `Mover`'s step (5 s) and on each cue.
    One timer per row (`asks`, `{:ask, name}`), so cues do not pile up timers.
  - Past the deadline it is `answered(…, {:refused, {:unknown_past_deadline, why}})`. On the
    target that is `back/3`: the act `failed` with reason
    `{:resume, {:unknown_past_deadline, why}}`, then the resume on the own account. That resume
    reads `usable:` by the same deadline, so an unknown there is `stranded/3`, reason
    `{:stranded, {:unknown_past_deadline, why}}`. A limited agent's is `left_gone/3`, as before.
  - The deadline is `move_deadline` (wall-clock ms), written with the phase `resuming` by a new
    `resuming_phase/3`. That happens at the copy, or at a way back from a refused copy (the row
    was never `resuming`). A way back from the resume keeps it, and the restart's `wake` reads
    it. The drain and the stop read `move_deadline` only before the copy (`past?` in `drain/2`
    and `stopping/3`), so the field means the deadline of the step under way.
    `Sessions.move_phase/5` takes `deadline:` beside `to:` and `act:`.
  - The option `resume_wait:` (ms, 5 min by default), and the config key `parts.mover.resume_wait`
    (`Config`'s `@parts`, `:ms`).
  - The headless own word's key is `move:<agent>:<move_deadline>`, now the resume's deadline. It
    is written before `own_word/2` runs and kept on a way back, so one move still stores one word.
- **The console line** (`Console.leases/2`): `lease 3: builder for app asked on work, seat 1: …`.
  The `@doc` says what the account is, with an example. The example's test matches
  `asked on one`.
- **Docs**: the design's "A lease", the `resuming` step, the own word's key and the failure list
  in "How `Mover` does it", and `Move.tla`'s entry in "The model". Also the `Mover`'s moduledoc
  and `start_link/1`, `Sessions.move_phase/5`, `Sessions.Table`'s `move_deadline`, and
  `Config.new/1`'s `parts`.

## What I ran into

- The test's clock is an `Agent` that holds a time, so a deadline on it never passes by itself.
  The first run waited 20 s and failed. The test now moves the clock and cues the `Mover`, as
  the "given up" test moves it for the drain's deadline.
- The first model left `ResumeWaitEnds` on in every configuration. That about doubled each one's
  distinct states (`Move_Design` 46,554 to 84,762; `Move_Headless` 1,181,553 to 2,324,649 in
  38 s, past the fast gate's 30 s). Only an unknown reads the deadline, so it is guarded by
  `Unsure`.
- `mix test` failed at first on plug's lock, as in F-210. I ran `mix deps.get` and a `--force`
  compile in both environments.
- A row a framework before this unit left in `resuming` carries the drain's deadline in
  `move_deadline`, usually past. An unknown there is taken as a refusal at its first ask. A move
  that was resuming when the new code started is rare, and it goes back or is left down for a
  person, so I left it.

## Findings

### new-1. The resume's other unknowns still have no deadline

What happened: only `usable:`'s unknown is bounded. Other unknowns of a move's resume still
leave the row `resuming`, its process gone, until the next cue: the app's `launch:` answering
`{:unknown, _}` ("a part the launch needs has not started"), and `Mailbox.launch/4`'s or the
own word's unknowns (`{:launch, why}`, `{:word, why}`, `:no_first_line`). They are not asked
again at the step either.
Why: the brief bounds the flag's unknown, which `Move.tla` models. The launch's unknowns are
left abstract there. A row still `sending` is bounded by `Mailbox`'s own deadline, but the
app's launch is not bounded by anything.
What I did: nothing.
What the design should change: decide whether the resume's deadline bounds every unknown of
the resume, all taken as a refusal and the move going back. One `unanswered/3` would serve all
of them. The other way is to say the app's launch answers unknown only for a bounded time.

### new-2. One deadline for the resume and its way back can strand at once

What happened: the target's unknown runs out at the deadline, and the way back reads the own
account by the same deadline, which has passed. If the own account's check cannot tell either,
often for the same cause (`claude auth status` failing for both), the row is left down at once,
with no wait on the way back.
Why: the brief says "back to its own account if that is usable, else the row left down". One
deadline written once per move keeps that bounded and lets a restart read it from the row.
What I did: built one deadline. The design and the moduledoc say a way back keeps it.
What the design should change: either nothing (the fault ledger's `stranded` act reaches a
person), or a way back after a deadline gets a `resume_wait` of its own. That would be a
second write of `move_deadline`, and the own word's key would then need another source.
