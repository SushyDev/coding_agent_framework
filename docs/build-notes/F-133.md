# F-133: R12, `Mover`: a session moved to another account

R12 (`docs/design.md`, "Moving it", "Ending a session", "Delivery", "The parts", item 5),
against `model/tla/Move.tla` as F-122 rewrote it and `Delivery.tla`, for main sessions and
delegates alike, with F-97's new-1, F-114's new-2, F-118's new-2, F-122's new-1 and F-130's
new-1 read for it.

## What was built

- **`caf_mover`**, a new app above `caf_mailbox`, with one module, `Mover`: one process per
  host, no table. `move/4` (actor, agent, `to:`, by default `Usage.pick/4` among the other
  accounts) claims the act `move` in `Acts` on the session's process and the number of its
  ended moves, marks it acted, and writes the phase on the agent row; it answers once the drain
  began. Each cue it declares (`sessions`, `mailbox`, `registry`, `stream`) and its deadline's
  one timer take every move under way one step, by the row's phase:
  - `draining`: clear once no `Mailbox` row to the session is `sending` and no `run_delegate`
    call of it waits (`Mailbox.awaited/3`, new);
  - `stopping`: `Sessions.stop/3` `for: :move` in a task; refused while a turn runs, it is asked
    again only on a `registry:` or `stream:` cue (see "What I ran into");
  - `copying`, once the row is free: the transcript and the directory of the same name beside
    it, each copied whole under a temporary name, renamed, and the size checked;
  - `resuming`: `Sessions.resume/6` on the other account with the same session id, and the
    app's launch for the row (`launch:`), without options so the row's own are taken again.
    Done when the row runs there: the act `verified`, then the fields cleared.
  - Failures, as the module doc and the design say: given up at the deadline before the
    SIGTERM (session on its own account, act `failed`); the old process ending by itself (the
    move goes on); a late stop (waits for `Recoveries`, then goes on); the copy or the resume
    refused on the other account (act `failed`, resumed on its own account); refused there too
    (`stranded`: a `failed` act in the fault ledger, the row `stopped`). A launch that answers
    `{:unknown, _}` is asked again at the next cue.
  - At its start it goes on from each row's phase, and settles `failed` (`interrupted`) a move
    act left acted with no row naming it.
- **`Sessions`**: the agent row's `move`, `move_from`, `move_to`, `move_act`, `move_deadline`
  (added columns; a start writes none, so a move's resume keeps them); `begin_move/4` (one write,
  refused while a move is under way, for a row not running, or to its own account);
  `move_phase/5`; `stop/3` `for: :move`, which ends a delegate by SIGTERM by its record once
  `Stream`'s fold, read then, says its turn ended and no row to it is `sending` (an
  `end_session` would have `Mailbox` settle its queued rows `ended`); `ended/3` refused while a
  move is under way. `Sessions.Agents.moving?/1` and `move/1`.
- **`Mailbox`**: no stream or waiter mark to an agent a move is moving (`:moving` target); a
  waiting call still takes its answer. `awaited/3`.
- **`Delegates`**: a start under a parent a move drains is refused `{:moving, parent}`
  (`NoStartWhileDraining`); a delegate a move is moving is not settled (its end is not a kill,
  and no `ended` is asked while it is free between the stop and the resume); `launch/2`, the
  launch its own resume uses, for the `Mover`.
- **`MCP`**: the tool `move(agent, to)`, checked by `Grants.may_fix?/3` (a parent's of its
  delegate, the operator's of any agent), then `mover:`'s `move/4`; `Operator.move/3`.
- **The example app** starts `Mover` after `Delegates`, hands `MCP` `{Mover, pid}`, and gives
  the launch: a delegate's from `Delegates.launch/2`, a main session's from the config, each
  with a new MCP config (`{:unknown, :no_mcp}` while `MCP` has not started after a restart).
- **Test aids**: the interactive stand-in takes `cut <path>` (on SIGTERM it first appends a
  line there, as a turn the SIGTERM cuts is kept in the transcript), and `WaiterStandIn.take/4`
  writes a take on its own.
- **Docs**: the design's parts table, the cue table, "How `Mover` does it" under "Moving it",
  the tools table and R12; `Move.tla`'s header; README's Status and "The apps" chart.

## What I ran into

- A stop refused while a turn runs writes the agent row twice (`stopping`, then `running`), and
  so cues `sessions:`, which the `Mover` reads. Asking the stop again at every cue loops. So a
  stop is asked when the drain clears, and again only on a reader's cue, when the turn's
  evidence can have changed; the deadline's timer and a restart count as one.
- `Acts` closes a `claimed` row of an earlier start as `interrupted`, never retried, while a
  move goes on after a restart. So the act is marked `acted` before the phase is written, and a
  `Mover` start settles an acted move act no row names.
- After a restart `MCP` starts after `Mover`, so a resume's launch cannot issue an MCP config
  yet; a launch's unknown answer waits for the next cue instead of sending the move back.
- A headless delegate's `end_session` settles its queued `Mailbox` rows `ended` once its process
  is gone, which a move must not: they are for the next process.

## Findings

### new-1. No reader of a session's background commands, so the drain does not wait for them

What happened: "Moving it" step 2 and `Move.tla`'s `Cleared` (`bg = {}`, with `NothingLost`)
wait until no background command runs. Nothing in the code reads a session's background
commands, and "Rules learned" says not to trust `background_tasks`.
Why: the brief named the drain without them; no part reads them.
What I did: the drain waits for no row sending and no call waiting; the idle read covers the
turn only. A background command the move's SIGTERM ends is lost, as `Break_Move_NoDrain` shows.
What the design should change: name the evidence of a running background command per pinned
Claude Code release (a transcript line, a file, a hook), and a reader of it that the drain waits
for; until then, say that a move with background commands running loses them.

### new-2. The move at 90 % of the allowance is not built

What happened: "Moving it" starts a move on its own at 90 %; the brief named only the act.
What I did: nothing; `Mover.move/4` with no `to:` already picks by `Usage`.
What the design should change: a unit where `Mover` reads `usage:` cues and moves each main
session of an account at 90 %, as actor `mover:<host>`, once per reading's state.

### new-3. The agent's model is not on the agent row

What happened: a resume needs `model:`, which `Sessions` does not keep; `Delegates` keeps a
delegate's, and nothing keeps a main session's.
What I did: `Delegates.launch/2` gives a delegate's; the example app's main launch names none,
so a moved main session takes its account's default model.
What the design should change: keep the model with the launch options on the agent row, so any
resume (a move, a recovery) gives the same launch without asking the part that started it.

### new-4. What else Claude Code keeps per session is not copied

What happened: the copy takes `<session id>.jsonl` and the directory of that name beside it.
Claude Code keeps other per-session files under its config directory (to-dos, file history,
session environment) that `--resume` may read.
What the design should change: the per-release check lists which files a `--resume` reads, and
the copy takes those.

### new-5. What state a move claims

What happened: the design says a fix acts once per state, not what a move's state is. A state
of the process alone would make a press after a move that was given up a second claim of it,
refused for good.
What I did: the state is the process (pid, start time, account) and the count of its moves
already ended; a second press while one runs is refused (`{:moving, phase}` by the row, or
`{:claimed, by}`).
What the design should change: confirm the state, or name another.

### new-6. R12 is one unit at about three times the ideal size

What happened: +1,631 −44 lines, half of it tests and docs.
Why: the phases and failures are one mechanism, but they touch `Sessions`, `Mailbox`,
`Delegates`, `MCP` and the app, and every property needs a test over the real parts.
What I did: kept it one unit, since a `Mover` without its drain in `Mailbox` and `Delegates`
would not hold `Move.tla`'s properties.
What the design should change: nothing, unless a later unit of this size should be split by
the order of the steps (the act and the drain; then the copy and the resume).

### new-7. A headless session's SIGTERM is not measured

What happened: a delegate's move ends it by SIGTERM; F-114 measured SIGTERM only for an
interactive session (exit 143, transcript kept, SessionEnd `other`).
What the design should change: the per-release check measures a headless session's SIGTERM:
its transcript kept, its log's last line whole, and its `--resume` then.

### new-8. A call's answer is still taken during the drain

What happened: the brief says the drain marks no new deliveries. A `run_delegate` call under
way when the drain began waits for its report, and the drain waits for that call; with the
take refused too, the drain would wait for the call's deadline (up to the tool timeout).
What I did: `Mailbox` marks no stream or waiter row to a moving agent, and a waiting call still
takes its answer, inside the turn that called.
What the design should change: confirm that reading of "no new deliveries".
